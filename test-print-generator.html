Riso Print Settings

PaperA4 or Letter, white uncoated (same stock you normally use)
DrumsPass 1: Blue  →  Pass 2: Yellow
Print orderBlue first (darker ink), yellow second. This matters — the order affects the overprint color.
SpeedSlow (1-2) for consistent ink density
Ink densityMedium-dark (3 out of 5, or your normal default). Do NOT set to maximum.
MasterStandard resolution (300-400 dpi). Use "Photo" mode if your riso has it.
RegistrationUse pin registration or careful manual alignment between passes
CopiesPrint 3-5 copies (first few sheets have inconsistent ink density as drum warms up)
DryingLet blue pass dry 10+ minutes before running yellow pass

Important: Feed the separation files as grayscale images. Black = full ink, white = no ink. The chart has 5 density levels per channel (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) giving 25 overprint combinations. Each patch is large enough to scan/photograph clearly. Labels are included in each plate.

After Printing

Scan/photographScan at 300+ dpi or photograph under consistent, neutral lighting (no warm/cool cast). Include a white paper border for white-balance reference.
What to compareFocus on the 5x5 grid center cells — especially the diagonal where both inks overlap at equal density (25/25, 50/50, 75/75, 100/100).
Key questionHow green vs. olive vs. muddy are the overlap patches? This tells us the opacity balance.
