Matrix preview (r=16, r=8) — the dither matrices themselves

Each pixel's brightness = its threshold value. Dark spots = cells that ink up first (dot centers). Bright spots = cells at Voronoi vertices (ink up last). Matches paper Fig. 4d.
How it works. The matrix is built once: cells are visited along the chosen path; each free cell becomes a dot center and marks all neighbors within radius r as occupied. Then every cell gets a threshold equal to its rank when sorted by distance-to-nearest-center (with wrap-around). Rendering is just bit = (1 − luminance) > threshold. Centers turn on first (small black dots in highlights), Voronoi vertices turn on last (small white dots in shadows). The 2:1 face-to-vertex ratio of the Delaunay triangulation gives the asymmetric highlight/shadow appearance for free.
